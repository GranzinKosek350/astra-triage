2. Paediatric early-warning score
   The banded instrument implemented in src/astra_triage/comparators/pews.py follows
   the published family of paediatric early-warning scores (heart rate, respiratory
   rate, systolic pressure, saturation, temperature and consciousness, scored against
   age bands). The article prints no band table, so the bands and the percentage
   deviation rule in that module are this release's implementation of the instrument
   and are stated as such in the module docstring.

3. Paediatric appendicitis score
   The eight-item, ten-point instrument in src/astra_triage/comparators/pas.py follows
   the published appendicitis score: migration, anorexia, nausea or vomiting, right
   lower quadrant tenderness, rebound or cough tenderness, temperature above 38 C,
   leukocytosis and a neutrophil shift. The item weights are the published ones.

4. Temporal Fusion Transformer
   The design implemented in src/astra_triage/comparators/tft.py follows Lim B,
   Arik SO, Loeff N, Pfister T. "Temporal Fusion Transformers for interpretable
   multi-horizon time series forecasting", International Journal of Forecasting 37
   (2021) 1748-1764. The implementation here is a single-logit adaptation written for
   this release; no code from the original is copied.

5. GRU-D
   The design implemented in src/astra_triage/comparators/grud.py follows Che Z,
   Purushotham S, Cho K, Sontag D, Liu Y. "Recurrent neural networks for multivariate
   time series with missing values", Scientific Reports 8 (2018). The implementation
   is written for this release.
